# Gesture specification

Priority is pause/safety, two-hand zoom, active drag, discrete click, scroll, pointer, then profile actions. Entry requires confidence 0.75 for 120 ms; staying requires 0.60. Right short fist (90–350 ms, fired on reopen) clicks; a fist held 450 ms starts left drag. Left open hand scrolls and a short left fist right-clicks. Two pinches zoom. Two still, wide open hands held 1,000 ms toggle pause. All thresholds are centralized and calibration-adjustable.
